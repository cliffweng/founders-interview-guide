# Mock Q packs (hotspots)
{: .no_toc }

*~9 min read*

**🎯 Interview frequent**

## Why it matters

Reading the spine and answering out loud are different skills. Founder interviews are short, interruptible, and allergic to a memorized script. This page is the drill: a YC-style pack and an early-VC pack, with the shape of a good answer and a pointer to the page that holds the substance. Run it with a cofounder or a friend who is willing to cut you off.

## Core concepts

- **How to run it.** Ten minutes for Pack A. One person asks, and they must interrupt at least three times. Record it. The only debrief that matters: which sentence broke (jargon, no user, a number you couldn't define, an answer longer than the question deserved). Fix that sentence and run it again. Don't add slides.
- **Pack B is thirty minutes.** Two minutes of narrative, then questions. The interviewer should ask for a definition and a customer by name or context. Debrief the same way, plus: did the ask land on a milestone?
- **Answer shape, every time.** Plain sentence. Past-tense fact. Ugly qualifier, said by you. Stop. The content lives in the earlier topics. If you "win" the mock by being charming and vague, the mock failed.
- **Don't memorize paragraphs.** Memorize the facts (the user, the numbers, the cut, the price, the split) and the order. Partners notice a recited answer, and an interruption destroys it.
- **Trade places.** The person who only asks never hears their own hedges. Both founders should be able to answer. A YC interview can aim the next question at either of you.

## Mental model

```
Timer on → they interrupt → you answer the new question → stop → after: which sentence broke
```

Silence while you recall a true number is better than a smooth sentence you have to retract.

## Interview questions

Pack A is the YC-style ten minutes. Pack B is the seed-style follow-up. Answer keys are the shape, not a script.

1. **Pack A — What do you do?**
   Answer: Two sentences a non-expert can picture, plus one person in a scene. No category nouns. Full treatment: [Product sense](../04-product-sense-prioritization/) and [Traps](../12-common-traps-weak-answers/).

2. **Pack A — Who is the user? Tell me about the last one.**
   Answer: Role and context, the last time the problem happened, the workaround, the cost, and what you changed. If you only have friends, say so. Full treatment: [Problem clarity](../03-problem-clarity-customer-stories/).

3. **Pack A — How do you make money, and what's the number?**
   Answer: Who pays, how much, how often. Then the real traction sentence: absolute level, dates, and the quality label (paid, pilot, LOI). Full treatment: [Pricing](../09-business-model-pricing/) and [Traction](../07-traction-metrics-under-fire/).

4. **Pack A — Why you, and why now?**
   Answer: One edge that is a fact, and one change that unlocked the product this year. "We care" and "AI is hot" don't count. Full treatment: [Why this / why now / why you](../02-why-this-why-now-why-you/).

5. **Pack A — What are you raising, if anything, and what do you do with it? Who else is on the team?**
   Answer: Amount, milestone, commitment constraint (including school). Equity only if they poke a weird split. Full treatment: [Fundraising](../11-fundraising-narrative-ask/) and [Team](../10-team-equity-roles/).

6. **Pack B — Walk the wedge, then the bigger market. What's unproven?**
   Answer: Bottom-up count for the first buyers, one adjacent step, and an explicit label on the step you have not sold. Full treatment: [Market size & wedge](../05-market-size-wedge/).

7. **Pack B — Who do you lose to today, and why doesn't the incumbent copy you?**
   Answer: Status quo plus a named alternative. One user-visible axis. A structural reason or an honest head start, not a moat you don't have. Full treatment: [Competition](../06-competition-differentiation/).

8. **Pack B — Define the metric. Show a cohort. What did you spend to get them?**
   Answer: Definition, absolutes, cohort, and "founder time" if you didn't pay for the channel. Recompute the growth rate with the interviewer. Full treatment: [Traction](../07-traction-metrics-under-fire/) and [Go-to-market](../08-go-to-market-story/).

9. **Pack B — Two-minute story, then: what has to be true in eighteen months for this round to have been right?**
   Answer: Narrative order, then a milestone a stranger could check. Not a valuation. Full treatment: [Fundraising](../11-fundraising-narrative-ask/).
